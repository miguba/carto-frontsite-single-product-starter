## Information we collect

We collect the contact, billing, delivery, order, and payment-related information needed to operate the store and fulfill purchases. Payment providers process payment credentials under their own privacy terms.

## How information is used

We use customer information to process orders, prevent fraud, provide support, meet legal obligations, and improve the service. We do not sell personal information.

## Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable law provides those rights. Contact the support address shown in the site footer.
